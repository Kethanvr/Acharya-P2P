# Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Alignment

Renewalytics is built on the foundation of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. Our platform directly contributes to four key global goals.

---

### 🟢 SDG 7: Affordable and Clean Energy
**Target**: Increase substantially the share of renewable energy in the global energy mix.
**How Renewalytics helps**:
- Provides a centralized platform to track the deployment and performance of solar, wind, and hydro energy.
- Makes renewable energy data accessible and transparent, encouraging further investment and deployment.

---

### 🔵 SDG 13: Climate Action
**Target**: Integrate climate change measures into national policies, strategies, and planning.
**How Renewalytics helps**:
- Quantifies the impact of renewable energy on carbon reduction.
- Provides data-driven justification for phasing out fossil-fuel-based power plants in regional districts.

---

### 🟠 SDG 9: Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure
**Target**: Upgrade infrastructure and retrofit industries to make them sustainable.
**How Renewalytics helps**:
- Uses **cutting-edge AI (Gemini 2.5 Flash)** to modernize energy management.
- Implements real-time telemetry simulators that showcase how "Smart Grids" can be monitored and optimized.

---

### 🟡 SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities
**Target**: Reduce the adverse per capita environmental impact of cities.
**How Renewalytics helps**:
- Empowers district-level leadership in Karnataka to plan for localized energy self-sufficiency.
- Helps optimize energy distribution within urban and rural clusters to reduce waste.

---

## The Vision
By 2030, we aim for Renewalytics to be the standard dashboard for every green-energy-conscious region, turning raw environmental data into a roadmap for a carbon-neutral future.
